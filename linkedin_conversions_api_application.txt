----------------------------------------------------------------------
Relay is a social media management SaaS platform that helps brands
and marketing teams plan, schedule, publish, and analyze content
across multiple social platforms including LinkedIn.

Our users (primarily SMBs and marketing agencies) run organic
LinkedIn content campaigns directly from Relay. We want to integrate
the LinkedIn Conversions API to give them a closed-loop view of
which posts and campaigns are actually driving business outcomes —
signups, form fills, demo requests, and purchases — not just
impressions and clicks.

Specifically, we plan to:

1. CAMPAIGN PERFORMANCE MEASUREMENT — Surface conversion data
   alongside organic post analytics inside Relay's Analytics
   dashboard. Users will see a "Conversions" column next to
   engagement metrics (likes, comments, shares) so they can
   measure the true ROI of each post and campaign.

2. CROSS-JOURNEY TRACKING — Many of our users run both organic
   LinkedIn posts and paid campaigns. We will use the Conversions
   API to unify online conversion events (website visits, signups)
   with the LinkedIn content that drove them, giving a full
   customer journey view across organic and paid touchpoints.

3. ALTERNATIVE TRACKING MECHANISM — Some of our users operate in
   regulated industries (finance, healthcare) where installing the
   LinkedIn Insight Tag directly on their website is restricted by
   IT or compliance policy. The server-side Conversions API lets
   these users still measure LinkedIn-driven conversions without
   a client-side tag dependency.

We will collect conversion events server-side via our NestJS
backend and forward them to LinkedIn's Conversions API using the
user's authenticated LinkedIn access token. All data handling
follows GDPR and CCPA requirements — we do not store or resell
user conversion data.
